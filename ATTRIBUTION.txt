This tool uses a subset of the MP3 samples, nearest-note pitch transposition,
amplitude-based velocity, a release envelope, compression and algorithmic reverb.
It does not implement the full original multi-velocity sample instrument.
The downloaded WAV contains an attribution comment; retain this credit when sharing.
