ORIGINAL MODULAR CITY KIT — 2026-10-27
Applies ONLY to: cyber-city-original.glb

Created for the YI-CHI KUO portfolio from newly authored procedural geometry
and algorithmically drawn raster textures. The complete reproducible generator
is tools/build-cyber-city.py (Python 3 standard library; no artwork inputs).

All 14 parts, window colour atlas, emissive atlas and black wall emission mask
are generated from numeric coordinates and pixel rules in that script.
No Cyberpunk 2077 or other game exports are used. No third-party models,
photographs, downloaded textures or AI-generated image textures are used.
The kit may be used, modified and published as part of this portfolio.

Textures are embedded in the GLB (256x256, 256x256 and 1x1 PNG).
Two shared opaque PBR materials; no normal maps. Windows are physically
offset 0.014 units from the supporting facade. No signs, text or logos.
Deterministic seed: 271026. Geometry is Y-up with bottom-centre pivots.

This is a newly generated asset, NOT a conversion or modification of any earlier model.
The earlier Cyber City (papptimus, CC BY 4.0) model has been removed from the site.

Also original (not part of the kit): district LED signs, arcade rides, car, metro, harbour details and lighting.
